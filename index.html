02 · Current Lead Gen — how Simplicity fills the calendar today

The engine that turns the recruiting promise into booked appointments right now, built primarily on Lone Beacon (acquired 2023) — a full-service marketing firm targeting the 50+ / pre-retiree demographic — plus turnkey workshops and the enablement stack.

The lead-gen machine (Lone Beacon)

  • Organic — SEO, content, email, websites
  • Qualification — first-party form data incl. net investable assets to target HNW; appointments handed to the advisor for final qualification

Workshop & specialty funnels

  • Turnkey educational workshops — Social Security, "Truth About Annuities," tax diversification; scripts, invites, pre/post analytics

Advisors buy marketing packages from ~$250/mo (website) up to ~$20,000/mo full retainer. It works — but lead volume is tied to ad spend and seminar cadence, cost-per-appointment is high, and the ad markets are competitive. That ceiling is exactly what Lead Gen Max is designed to break.

Tab 3 · The End Game

03 · Lead Gen Max — an owned, AI-leveraged appointment engine

Instead of renting leads, manufacture qualified appointments at low marginal cost: source public/business data on newly-liquid, high-intent prospects, enrich it, run AI-personalized multi-touch outreach, and distribute booked appointments to advisors — with cost limited to third-party subscriptions (in-house build, ~$0 dev).

The pipeline

Source→ Enrich→ AI outreach→ Book→ Distribute→ Track
  • Source — e.g. SEC Form D filings (newly-liquid business owners/investors) + other public/business signals, with a Source badge per record.
  • Enrich — append contact + wealth signals; per-field readiness indicators show what's present vs. still needed.
  • AI outreach — personalized cold email / multi-touch sequences.
  • Distribute — two-stage assignment of booked appointments to the right advisor.

Phased rollout

  • MVP — one source + basic enrichment + manual send
  • TurboCharge — automated multi-touch + richer enrichment
  • Go-Max — full pipeline, server-side ingestion, live distribution

Cost model across all phases = 3rd-party subscriptions only (data / enrichment / sending). In-house build = $0 dev.

Concept 02 · Client-Facing

01 · The Simplicity Client Center (SCC) — the unified digital home for every client

The client-facing counterpart to Client Acquisition. SCC is the AI-driven Intelligence Hub that unifies every Simplicity capability into a single, intelligent, proactive "One Simplicity" experience — the digital bridge connecting a client's financial future to the collective power of Simplicity Group and its agents.

Pillar 1

One Simplicity Experience

Replaces disparate client views across all affiliates with a single, intuitive platform. Centralizes the brand and accelerates M&A value capture.

Pillar 2

Intelligent Financial Profiling

AI-driven onboarding understands each client's needs, goals, and full financial position — not just account data. Drives organic growth by surfacing cross-sell opportunities.

Pillar 3

Holistic Access

Personalized visibility & education across the Six Fundamental Considerations — Longevity, Liquidity, Inflation, Market, Mortality, Taxes.

Why it matters — the strategic case

  • Brand centralization — one "One Simplicity" home regardless of which affiliate the client started with.
  • M&A value capture — every acquired book plugs into a single client experience.
  • Organic growth — the profile + AI engine continuously surface cross-sell/gap opportunities.
  • Agent as revenue driver — client activity flows straight back into the Agent Center pipeline.

Meeting the 2026 market shift

By 2026 mobile is the #1 channel for investing. A "learn, enroll, service" app moves Simplicity from legacy provider → Digital Destination. Competitive landscape: none offer all-in-one intelligence + education + enrollment + servicing + agent contact.

Concept 02 · The Brains

04 · AI Engine (SIRE) & Agent Safeguards

SIRE — Simplicity Intelligent Recommendation Engine

An AI microservice that takes the client's financial profile as input and outputs Simplicity assessments & solutions. It powers the Action Center, Suggested Solutions, and the filtered catalog.

Gap Analysis

Flags under-insurance or excess volatility exposure; surfaces it in the Action Center.

Goal-Based Matching

"Retirement Security" → annuities; "Wealth Transfer" → life/estate tools.

Risk Calibration

Filters out products too aggressive/conservative for the client's tolerance.

Real-Time Optimization

Monitors market & life changes; re-aligns strategy and alerts client/agent.

Agent Center integration — SCC as a revenue driver

  • "Invite Client to SCC" button in the Agent Center Quick Links (launches the Scenario-B invite).
  • Client actions (start e-app, e-sign) flow instantly into the agent's "My Business" Pending pipeline.
  • Issued policies update the agent's Annuity & Life Target Premium metrics — ties SCC directly to Concept 01's recruiting pitch.

Safeguarding the primary agent

  • Primary Agent Sovereignty — AI never cross-sells or suggests a partner agent without the primary agent's digital sign-off.
  • Agent Approval Queue — AI-identified solutions route to the agent's dashboard before appearing in the client's Suggested Solutions.
  • Intelligent Collaborative Matching — needs outside the agent's specialty trigger a best-fit Simplicity partner/IMO "warm handoff," with the primary agent kept central.
  • Client distribution — unassigned (open-market) clients are matched to an agent by product need/profile.

Concept 03 · Executive

01 · How Simplicity grows — the buy-and-build value engine

Founded 2016, Simplicity has completed 70+ acquisitions, rolling up IMOs, BGAs, RIAs, marketing/data agencies and insurtech onto one operating platform under an employee-ownership partnership. Value compounds through a repeatable flywheel and is periodically monetized via PE recapitalizations.

The value flywheel

Acquire (tuck-in)→ Integrate · One Simplicity→ Cross-sell + scale→ Higher EBITDA & multiple→ Recap / liquidity→ Fund next deals

Small firms are bought at low multiples, re-valued at the platform multiple after integration, and the aggregate is monetized at each recap — value created on top of organic growth.

What they buy

The full distribution stack

IMOs/FMOs (InsurMark, IDA, Marketing Financial), BGAs (Northeast, LeSourd, Source — disability), retail broker-dealer (The Leaders Group — VUL/securities), RIA/wealth & fee-based (RetireOne), marketing/data (Lone Beacon), insurtech (Plum Life→LifeLink), asset managers (Verity). A product-agnostic distribution "supermarket."

Why (target profile)

Relationship-rich & recurring

Recurring commission revenue, retained producers, diversified carriers, product/geographic gap-fill, capability adds (marketing/data/tech), and founders wanting liquidity + equity (succession) who fit the partnership culture.

Integration

"One Simplicity" platform

Acquired firms migrate onto shared ops/finance/tech, led by EVP of Operations Heather Ulz (200+ ops staff). Central challenge: heterogeneous carriers, CRMs and enrollment systems — the reason for the LifeLink / point-of-sale standardization push.

Cadence

Accelerating pace

2 (’16) → 4 (’17) → 4 (’19) → 4 (’20) → 15 (’21) → 20 (’22) → ~20 (’23) → LifeLink & recap (’24) → 200+ new partners equitized (’25–’26). 100+ partner agencies · 20,000+ producers.

Concept 03 · The Decision

02 · The Flag Engine — how a target is judged

The screening logic leadership uses to decide buy vs. pass. The key insight is the Yellow tier: weaknesses that look like discounts but which Simplicity's platform can turn green — so the acquirer captures the upside rather than paying for it.

🟢 GREEN — pursue

  • Recurring / renewal commission revenue
  • Producer retention >90%; deep bench
  • Diversified carriers (top carrier <50%)
  • Product / geographic gap-fill
  • Clean compliance & E&O record
  • Healthy margin + organic growth
  • Motivated seller / succession + culture fit

🟡 YELLOW — fixable (turn green)

  • Weak back office → One Simplicity ops
  • Weak marketing/lead-gen → Lone Beacon
  • Manual quote/enroll → LifeLink / SCC
  • Founder succession gap → equity/partnership
  • Single-product focus → cross-sell shelf
  • Limited carrier access → carrier network

🔴 RED — avoid / discount

  • Carrier concentration (one = most revenue)
  • Producer / key-person concentration
  • Declining production
  • Compliance actions / litigation / E&O
  • Culture misfit / won't integrate
  • Thin or negative margins
  • Unrealistic price expectations

Valuation drivers (what lifts the multiple)

Scale% recurring revenueEBITDA marginOrganic growthRetention 90%+Carrier/product breadthCross-sell attachData/tech assetsManagement depth

Multiple arbitrage — worked example

Buy a $5M-EBITDA broker at ~10x = $50M; integrate onto the platform; re-value the same cash flow at the ~17x platform multiple = $85M → ~$35M created, before organic upside and synergies. Tuck-ins clear ~7.5–12x; platforms ~14x base (~19x with earn-outs).

Concept 03 · The Build

04 · Simplicity Vantage — the executive portal

A secure, board/executive portal that answers two questions at a glance: "What is Simplicity worth today, and where is it trending?" and "What should we acquire next?" — the second powered by an AI acquisition-discovery engine. Use the "Launch the demo →" button above to open the clickable prototype.

1 · The value dashboard (what leadership sees first)

  • Enterprise value + implied multiple + trajectory since 2016 (marking each recap)
  • Revenue, Adj. EBITDA & margin, organic vs. total growth
  • Scale & production — producers, partner agencies, annuity/life sales, AUM
  • Capital & ownership — sponsor, employee-ownership %, net leverage
  • Portfolio health — each division with 2–3 KPIs + integration status

2 · Acquisition Radar (the Opportunities engine)

A ranked, filterable list of AI-discovered targets — each with a Fit Score, Green/Yellow/Red summary, sale status and a recommended action (Pursue / Watch / Pass). Click one → a near-full-screen deep-dive: overview, key people, 3-yr financials, dependencies & concentration, 3rd-party relationships, full flag analysis, and the multiple-arbitrage rationale.

How the AI discovery works (the pipeline)

Flag Engine (G/Y/R criteria)→ Scheduled agent teams scan public data→ Consolidation agent ranks & best-fits→ Opportunities auto-populated→ Executive deep-dive modal
  • Train: the Flag Engine encodes leadership's Green/Yellow/Red criteria (Tab 2).
  • Hunt: each agent team is assigned a target type / flag category and mines publicly accessible data (state DOI licenses, SEC Form ADV, CMS/Medicare data, LinkedIn, filings, news, web signals) on a nightly schedule.
  • Consolidate: when all teams finish, a consolidation agent scores fit, removes duplicates, and assembles the best portfolio matches.
  • Surface: matches auto-populate the Radar for leadership to review and action.

Concept only — discovery uses public data; no live data or outreach is wired. Compliance & data-source review required before any build.
