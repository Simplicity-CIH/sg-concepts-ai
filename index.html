02 · Current Lead Gen — how Simplicity fills the calendar today

The engine that turns the recruiting promise into booked appointments right now, built primarily on Lone Beacon (acquired 2023) — a full-service marketing firm targeting the 50+ / pre-retiree demographic — plus turnkey workshops and the enablement stack.

The lead-gen machine (Lone Beacon)

  • Paid digital — Facebook, Google, LinkedIn
  • Broadcast — radio & TV
  • Events — live seminars & webinars (real-time promotion + multi-channel push)
  • Organic — SEO, content, email, websites
  • Qualification — first-party form data incl. net investable assets to target HNW; appointments handed to the advisor for final qualification

Workshop & specialty funnels

  • Turnkey educational workshops — Social Security, "Truth About Annuities," tax diversification; scripts, invites, pre/post analytics
  • Specialty programs — Tax-Free Retirement; Asset-Based LTC
  • Enablement wrap — Advanced Sales Training, Simplicity University®, Kai-Zen® Masterclass, Studio One video

Economics & the ceiling (why "Max")

Advisors buy marketing packages from ~$250/mo (website) up to ~$20,000/mo full retainer. It works — but lead volume is tied to ad spend and seminar cadence, cost-per-appointment is high, and the ad markets are competitive. That ceiling is exactly what Lead Gen Max is designed to break.

Tab 3 · The End Game

03 · Lead Gen Max — an owned, AI-leveraged appointment engine

Instead of renting leads, manufacture qualified appointments at low marginal cost: source public/business data on newly-liquid, high-intent prospects, enrich it, run AI-personalized multi-touch outreach, and distribute booked appointments to advisors — with cost limited to third-party subscriptions (in-house build, ~$0 dev).

The pipeline

Source→ Enrich→ AI outreach→ Book→ Distribute→ Track
  • Source — e.g. SEC Form D filings (newly-liquid business owners/investors) + other public/business signals, with a Source badge per record.
  • Enrich — append contact + wealth signals; per-field readiness indicators show what's present vs. still needed.
  • AI outreach — personalized cold email / multi-touch sequences.
  • Distribute — two-stage assignment of booked appointments to the right advisor.

Phased rollout

  • MVP — one source + basic enrichment + manual send
  • TurboCharge — automated multi-touch + richer enrichment
  • Go-Max — full pipeline, server-side ingestion, live distribution

Cost model across all phases = 3rd-party subscriptions only (data / enrichment / sending). In-house build = $0 dev.
